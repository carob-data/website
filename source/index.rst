Carob
=====

.. raw:: html

   </div>
   <div style="visibility: visible;">

.. image:: /_static/carob.png
  :width: 150
  :alt: CAROB logo
  :align: left

*Carob* produces large standardized data sets to facilitate agricultural research. It is an open-source, collaborative, and community based `project <introduction.html>`__. All data transformations are done with *R* scripts, making it easy to enhance the standardization process as needs arise, and to correct mistakes. Please consider `contributing <contribute/index.html>`_! 

| 
 
*Carob* is managed by Robert J. Hijmans (University of California, Davis) and Oscar Bautista Cespedes (International Institute of Tropical Agriculture).


Please use this `contact form <contact.html>`__ for questions and suggestions.
